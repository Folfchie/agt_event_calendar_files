# Happy Louyen Day
Join the **Alliance of Galactic Travellers** in the observance of our 9th annual **Happy Louyen Day** celebration.
## Overview
**Happy Louyen Day** is the anniversary of the first AGT settlers' arrival in the Louyen region of Hilbert Dimension on AGT Stardate 3733.23.09.

Louyen was the main capital of the AGT during the Atlas Rises era. While the region is significantly less populated now, there remains a small set of bases in the region from returning longtime AGT members.

The system data in the AGT Galactic Archives is currently being updated and is expected to complete by mid-2026.
## Details
All AGT Travellers, Visitors, and Diplomats are invited to attend the event.

We will be hosting the following activities:
- Exocraft Races
- Xeno Arena Battles
- Corvette Showcase

Other details, including the event schedule and location, are posted on the **AGT Special Events Calendar**.
